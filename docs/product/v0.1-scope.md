# v0.1 Working Scope

## Status and release intent

v0.1 is a working product boundary for a usable dogfood release. It is not an approved technical architecture. Jobs are the primary supported Opportunity type; future Opportunity types are deliberately not modeled in detail.

## Goals

- Help a Candidate discover distinct job Opportunities from a small set of structured sources.
- Answer which Opportunities deserve attention today.
- Evaluate Eligibility before Fit, then assess Opportunity Quality.
- Explain blockers, supporting signals, uncertainty, and ranking reasons.
- Preserve Candidate Evidence and user decisions through a basic application pipeline.
- Remain compatible with local/self-hosted use while architecture is decided later.

## In scope

### Candidate

- Career Profile
- skills, experience, and projects
- basic Evidence
- Opportunity and search Preferences

### Discovery

- Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever source adapters
- normalized Opportunity representation
- deduplication
- background scanning

### Intelligence

- Eligibility analysis and hard-block detection
- Fit analysis
- Opportunity Quality analysis
- explainable ranking
- explicit uncertainty representation

### User workflow

- Opportunity discovery and detail
- save, shortlist, investigate, and dismiss actions
- basic application pipeline with durable state

### Interface and operations

- first-class web dashboard
- a basic API needed to support the web application
- local/self-hosted-friendly operation
- background workers and scanning
- eventual containerized development and deployment

This scope does not select implementation technology. Separate architecture and implementation ADRs now select the initial runtime, frameworks, repository, and persistence approach without changing these product outcomes. Cloud, container, and AI provider/model choices remain open.

## Out of scope for v0.1

These remain possible later unless a future decision changes direction:

- automatic job submission or mass application
- Gmail automation
- interview agent or mock interviews
- offer negotiation
- recruiter or contact discovery
- deep company intelligence
- dozens of Opportunity source integrations
- browser automation for arbitrary career sites
- comprehensive country-specific immigration databases
- outcome-trained recommendation calibration
- personalized learning plans
- native mobile apps
- hosted SaaS
- plugin marketplace
- complete MCP implementation
- complete public SDK
- advanced multi-user collaboration

## v0.1 workflow

```text
Career Profile → Preferences → Discover → Normalize + Deduplicate
→ Eligibility → Fit → Opportunity Quality → Rank → Dashboard
→ Shortlist / Investigate / Dismiss → Application Pipeline
```

## Acceptance-level product outcomes

At a product level, v0.1 is successful when:

- A Candidate can create a useful Career Profile, Evidence record, and Preferences without fabricated fields.
- The system can discover and deduplicate job Opportunities from the three planned ATS sources.
- Each evaluated Opportunity presents Eligibility, Fit, and Quality as distinct concepts.
- Hard blockers and unknowns are visible, with unknown never silently converted to no.
- The Candidate can understand why an Opportunity was ranked and inspect the underlying signals.
- The Candidate can save, shortlist, investigate, or dismiss an Opportunity.
- A shortlisted Opportunity can enter a durable basic application pipeline.
- No external application is submitted without deliberate human action.
- The core workflow can remain viable for local/self-hosted operation.

These outcomes intentionally avoid prescribing implementation details or a final scoring model.

## Risks

- Source data may be incomplete, inconsistent, stale, or ambiguous.
- Sponsorship and authorization language may be absent or difficult to interpret.
- Overconfident Eligibility decisions could wrongly exclude valuable Opportunities.
- Fit and Quality could drift into an opaque combined score.
- Candidate Evidence may be incomplete or difficult to verify.
- Deduplication may merge distinct Opportunities or retain duplicates.
- Background scanning may create cost and freshness tradeoffs.
- A dashboard-first implementation could accidentally embed intelligence logic in the interface.
- Scope may expand into autonomous application behavior before the core is trustworthy.

## Unresolved questions

- What exact Eligibility states and transitions should v0.1 expose?
- Which signals are hard blockers, conditional blockers, or investigation prompts?
- How should confidence, missing information, and conflicting evidence be represented?
- What is the initial Fit and Opportunity Quality rubric, and how is ranking explained without collapsing the concepts?
- What constitutes verified Evidence, and how is provenance maintained through edits?
- What normalization and deduplication rules are reliable across the three ATS sources?
- What application pipeline states are required for dogfooding?
- What freshness, legitimacy, and red-flag checks are appropriate in v0.1?
- What exact job states, lease/concurrency values, physical schema details, provider capabilities, and provider/model configuration should implementation prove?
- Where should local/self-hosted boundaries end if a hosted offering is considered later?
- What privacy, deletion, export, and external-processing controls are required before implementation?

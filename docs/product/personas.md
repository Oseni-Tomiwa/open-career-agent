# Initial Personas

These personas describe product needs, not demographic profiles. A real Candidate may match several at once.

## International Candidate requiring sponsorship or relocation

### Goals

- Find Opportunities they can realistically pursue across regions.
- Understand sponsorship, authorization, remote geography, and relocation constraints.
- Focus effort where missing information can be investigated productively.

### Problems

- Job posts often omit or ambiguously describe sponsorship and location rules.
- Strong Fit can mask an Eligibility blocker.
- Country-specific assumptions create false positives and false negatives.

### Important product needs

- Eligibility separated from Fit and Quality.
- Explicit hard blockers, unknowns, and sources for conclusions.
- Geography, authorization, sponsorship, relocation, language, citizenship, and clearance signals.
- An investigate action for decision-relevant missing information.

## Early-career developer

### Goals

- Identify attainable roles with genuine growth potential.
- Present projects, education, and emerging skills truthfully.
- Understand gaps without being excluded solely by noisy requirements.

### Problems

- Limited formal experience makes keyword-only matching unreliable.
- Seniority and experience requirements can be ambiguous.
- Application advice may invent or exaggerate accomplishments.

### Important product needs

- Career Profile enrichment grounded in projects, education, and verified Evidence.
- Explainable Fit across skills, experience, seniority, project relevance, and direction.
- Clear distinction between a blocker, a gap, and an unknown.
- Evidence-backed application preparation in later releases.

## Experienced Candidate optimizing a high-volume search

### Goals

- Reduce time spent reviewing low-value Opportunities.
- Compare Opportunities consistently across sources.
- Track applications and eventually learn which strategies work.

### Problems

- Duplicate and stale postings create review overhead.
- A single opaque match score hides tradeoffs.
- Application state and outcomes are easily fragmented across tools.

### Important product needs

- Background discovery, normalization, and deduplication.
- Separate Eligibility, Fit, and Opportunity Quality explanations.
- Efficient shortlist, investigate, dismiss, and pipeline workflows.
- Durable outcomes that can support a future feedback loop.

## Privacy-conscious self-hosting user

### Goals

- Retain control of sensitive career information.
- Run the system locally or in infrastructure they control.
- Avoid unnecessary dependency on a single provider.

### Problems

- Career tools may centralize detailed personal and employment data.
- Provider lock-in can make data portability and auditing difficult.
- Automated actions can expose information without adequate review.

### Important product needs

- Candidate data ownership, transparent processing, and human control.
- Local/self-hosted-friendly operation.
- Provider independence where practical.
- Clear provenance and boundaries for external data or AI processing.
